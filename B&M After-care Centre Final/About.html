About:

B&M After-care Centre was established in 2012 in Hillbrow, Johannesburg, in response to the growing need for a safe and supportive environment for children after school. Many children in the community were left without supervision, making them vulnerable to negative influences and unsafe surroundings. Recognising this challenge, our founder, Mr. Malesela Maumela, established B&M After-care Centre as a community-driven initiative dedicated to protecting and empowering young people. What began as a small safe space has grown into a nurturing environment where children can learn, play, receive guidance, and build the confidence needed to achieve a brighter future under the mentorship of positive role models. In its early years, the initiative faced significant financial challenges due to limited sponsorship and resources. Despite these obstacles, the commitment to serving the community remained unwavering. Through educational activities, recreational programmes, and a feeding scheme, B&M After-care Centre brought children together, fostered meaningful relationships, and provided hope and support to families within the community.

Mission:

To provide a safe, caring, and inclusive environment where children receive quality after-school care, educational support, nutritious meals, and positive mentorship that promotes their academic, social, emotional, and personal development.

Vision:

To be a leading community-based day-care centre that empowers every child with the knowledge, values, and opportunities needed to become confident, responsible, and successful individuals who positively contribute to their families and communities.
